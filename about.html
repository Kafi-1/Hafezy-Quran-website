About the project

A Quran companion, built with care.

Hafezy Quran aims to make Quran reading and recitation support accessible through an Android application.

Our purpose

The purpose of Hafezy Quran is to offer a focused digital Quran experience with reading, audio and Bangla pronunciation support. Please refer to the installed app for the exact capabilities available in your release.

Who it is for

It is intended for people who want a convenient Quran reading companion, including readers who find Bangla-script pronunciation helpful. Pronunciation text is not a substitute for learning correct recitation from a qualified teacher.
